Welcome

InTouch Foundation is a registered charity and is a community based effort to address the plight of homeless men and women on the streets of Yorkshire. Our dual purpose is to bring attention to this plight and provide an organisation where volunteers can become directly involved in the preparation and feeding of the less fortunate that exist on the edge of our society. As a not for profit organisation, we are able to accept charitable donations and use them to deliver hot meals and emotional support to those men and women directly on the streets – wherever the need is greatest.

All Food serving sessions - Individuals must be 18 + to attend.
