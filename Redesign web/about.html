About InTouch Foundation

InTouch Foundation is run by volunteers who tirelessly work on a voluntary basis to help sustain and succeed in the work that we do.

We have operated this policy since our launch in 2012, our founder Osman Gondal was keen to ensure that we operate on a 100% donation policy. We are proud to say that we remain consistent in this and hope to continue with this policy.

We do however have one paid employee and that is our driver, who works for InTouch Foundation on a part time basis.
