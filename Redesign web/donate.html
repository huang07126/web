Donate

Your donation will enable our work to continue, together we can find new ways to prevent homelessness and feed the hungry.

What your donation pays for

We publish the average cost of each part of our work, so you can see where your money goes.

£2.50

Pays for one hot meal, served on a mobile kitchen route.

Give £2.50

£5 / month

Sponsors two meals every month and helps us plan ahead.

Give £5 monthly

£30

Funds an emergency winter pack (blanket, hat, gloves, hot meal).

Give £30

Other ways to give

Standing order

Set up a regular gift directly through your bank. Get in touch and we will send you the details.

Payroll giving

Give a tax-free amount each month from your salary. We can write to your employer to set this up.

Leave a gift in your will

A gift in your will helps us plan for the long term. Our team can talk you through the options.

In memory

Many people give to InTouch in memory of a loved one. We can set up a personal tribute page.
