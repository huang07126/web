Volunteer with us

Volunteers are the heart of InTouch Foundation. Whether you can offer one evening a month or several shifts a week, there is a role that fits.

Volunteer roles

Mobile kitchen team

Help prepare meals, ride along on the kitchen van and serve food on the routes. The most direct way to meet the people we support.

Time: One evening shift, about 4 hours.

Kitchen prep volunteer

Work in the kitchen at our Bradford base preparing meals during the afternoon. Great if you prefer not to be out on the streets.

Time: Afternoon shifts, about 3 hours.

Driver

Drive the mobile kitchen van on its route. A clean licence and willingness to drive in winter conditions are essential.

Time: One evening shift, about 4 hours.

Community advocate

Speak about our work at schools, faith groups, workplaces and community events. We provide a short induction and slide deck.

Time: Flexible.

Fundraiser

Help organise a coffee morning, sponsored walk, quiz night or other fundraising event. We support you all the way.

Time: Flexible.

Admin support

Help with data entry, donor letters, the volunteer rota and other behind-the-scenes work. Can be done from home.

Time: A few hours a week.

How sign-up works

  1. Fill in the volunteer interest form below.
  2. Our coordinator Priya will email you within five working days.
  3. You will be invited to a friendly one-hour induction at our Bradford base.
  4. Once you have completed the induction, you can pick shifts from our weekly rota.
